About StayNear

StayNear is a student accommodation website designed to help students find suitable places to live while studying in Freetown.

Finding a safe and affordable place to stay can be difficult for students, especially those who are new to the area. StayNear makes the search easier by allowing students to explore accommodation in locations such as Lumley, Congo Cross and Wilberforce.

Students can filter accommodation by location, budget and room type. They can also view accommodation details to help them compare their options and choose a place that suits their needs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding student accommodation simpler, more convenient and less stressful.
